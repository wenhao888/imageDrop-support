# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026

Welcome to imageDrop. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how imageDrop and its macOS companion application, imageDrop Bridge, handle your information when you use our applications.

## 1. About imageDrop

imageDrop is an application that allows users to scan documents, capture photos, and transfer images and files from an iPhone to a Mac using a paired-device connection.

imageDrop consists of:

- **imageDrop for iOS:** Used to scan documents, capture photos, select files, and initiate transfers.
- **imageDrop Bridge for macOS:** A companion application that receives files from paired iPhones.

## 2. Information We Access and Process

### Photos, Documents, and Files

When you use imageDrop, the application may access photos, camera images, scanned documents, and files that you choose to transfer.

This information is used solely to provide the application's scanning, capture, and file-transfer functionality.

Your files are transferred to your paired Mac and stored in the destination folder configured by imageDrop Bridge.

We do not use your personal documents or photos for advertising or sell them to third parties.

### Camera and Photo Library Access

imageDrop may request permission to access your camera or photo library.

- Camera access is used to capture photos and scan documents.
- Photo library access is used to let you select photos for transfer.

You can manage these permissions through your iPhone's Settings.

### Device Pairing and Connection Information

When pairing your iPhone with a Mac, imageDrop may process technical information required to establish and maintain the connection, such as:

- Device pairing information.
- Network connection information, including IP addresses.
- Connection and certificate information used by the pairing and transfer process.

This information is used to establish connections between your paired devices and support file transfers.

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

Information accessed or processed by imageDrop is used to:

- Capture photos and scan documents.
- Select and prepare files for transfer.
- Pair your iPhone with your Mac.
- Transfer files to your paired Mac.
- Maintain and troubleshoot device connections.

We do not use your documents, photos, or transferred files to build advertising profiles.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

imageDrop is designed for direct file transfers between your iPhone and your paired Mac.

Transferred files are stored in the uploads folder configured by imageDrop Bridge on your Mac.

Unless otherwise stated by a feature you choose to use, imageDrop does not upload your transferred documents or photos to an imageDrop-operated cloud storage service.

Files remain on your devices until you delete them.

You can delete transferred files from the uploads folder on your Mac. You can also remove locally stored application data by deleting the relevant files or uninstalling the application, subject to any files you have separately saved outside the application.

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your documents, photos, or transferred files with advertisers or data brokers.

Your files are transferred to the Mac that you pair with imageDrop. Anyone with access to that Mac or its configured uploads folder may be able to access the transferred files.

If future versions integrate third-party services, analytics, or other data-processing providers, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant practices.

## 6. Network Connections and Security

imageDrop uses network connections to communicate between your iPhone and your paired Mac.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the applications. Users should keep their devices and operating systems updated and only pair with trusted computers.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on your iPhone, Mac, and local network.

## 7. Permissions and Your Choices

You can control the permissions granted to imageDrop through your iPhone settings, including:

- Camera access.
- Photo library access.
- Local network access, where applicable.

You may revoke these permissions at any time. Some application features may not work without the permissions they require.

## 8. Children's Privacy

imageDrop is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through an imageDrop-operated account or online service.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to imageDrop, our data-handling practices, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
